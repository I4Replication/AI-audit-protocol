ones that could change its impact or wording. Check that the comment states observable discrepancies,
does not infer intent, calibrates impact to conclusions the paper actually draws, acknowledges

Audit privacy claims and the audit's own outputs especially carefully. Test the stated threat model,
the paper/archive wording, and live licence/terms where relevant. Search `source/context/` and the proposed
release artifacts for disclosed participant or quasi-identifying data; validate search patterns on

removed while the finding stays. Never request that a correct disclosure finding be removed,
deferred, coarsened out of the comment or moved to a private channel. A disclosure finding the
deposit does not support is REFUTED like any other claim, and BLOCKING when it is materially wrong.
